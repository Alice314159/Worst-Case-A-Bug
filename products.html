ReadShot

看到值得记的句子，拍一张就好。App 把文字取出来、帮你读懂，再排进复习队列——不用再截一堆图存在相册里发霉。 See a sentence worth keeping? Take a photo. ReadShot pulls the text out, helps you read it, and queues it for review — instead of screenshots rotting in your camera roll.

Sona

听懂 → 开口说 → 存下来复习 → 再往上走一层。图标里那两个气泡就是这个循环：语言不是背出来的，是用出来的。 Listen, say it out loud, save it, come back stronger. The two bubbles in the icon are that loop — a language gets used into place, not memorised.

Lexigo

背单词最大的敌人是无聊。Lexigo 把它做成一局一局能玩下去的小游戏，赢一局顺手记住几个词。 Boredom kills vocabulary apps. Lexigo turns it into short rounds you'll actually play — and you pick up a few words winning them.

MedTime

给家里人做的：几种药、几点吃、还剩多少，都替他们记着。提醒要足够明显，操作要足够少。 Built for family: which pills, what time, how many left — all remembered for them. Loud enough to notice, simple enough to use.

PhotoFlow

为 macOS 打造的本地照片挑选工具。快速浏览 JPEG、HEIC 和相机 RAW，读取拍摄信息，通过搜索、筛选和智能分组找到想要的照片，再用星级、颜色与旗标完成挑片。原片保持不变，标记保存在本机。 A local photo culling tool for macOS. Browse JPEG, HEIC and camera RAW files, inspect capture details, find shots with search, filters and smart groups, then cull with stars, color labels and flags. Originals stay untouched; marks are stored locally.

voCut

从录音、降噪、转写到剪辑导出，一套流程处理播客素材。可按文字定位片段、复核重复语句建议，也能重录不满意的段落；每个删除决定都由你确认。 Take podcast audio from recording and denoising through transcription and editing. Find passages by text, review repeated-phrase suggestions, and rerecord sections you want to improve. You approve every cut.

attentionStudio

一条视频最终是由注意力生成的——所以它管理的不是文件，是 prompt 本身：按分镜拆开，每镜一条，版本并排比，生成结果挂在对应版本下，好用的存成模板。 A video is ultimately generated out of attention — so this manages prompts, not files: broken down shot by shot, revisions side by side with their outputs attached, the good ones saved as templates.
