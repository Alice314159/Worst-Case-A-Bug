小棕瓶：美式医疗的终点在超市药房The Little Brown Bottle: Healthcare Ended at the Supermarket Pharmacy

问诊时，医生问常用哪家 Pharmacy。这个问题让我意识到，看病并不会在离开诊室时完整结束。还有一部分过程，要交给生活里另一个地方。During the consultation, the doctor asked which pharmacy we used. The question made me realize that leaving the examination room would not complete the process. Another part would happen somewhere else in daily life.

电子处方发到药房，我们再去拿药。药房在超市里，和日常采购处在同一片空间。身体的不舒服经过医生的询问与判断，最后又回到推着购物车、寻找一个窗口的普通生活中。The electronic prescription went to the pharmacy, and we went there to collect it. The pharmacy was inside a supermarket, in the same space as ordinary shopping. After the doctor's questions and judgment, the body's discomfort returned to an everyday task: finding a counter.

报姓名、生日、保险，一次次确认自己是谁，确认这份药属于谁。到这一步，我已经见过不少系统怎样认人。可当身份核对与身体联系在一起，那些熟悉的问题又显出另一层具体。Name, date of birth, insurance. Again and again, we confirmed who we were and whom the medicine belonged to. By then I had seen many ways systems identified people. These familiar questions became concrete in another way when they concerned the body.

最后拿到的是贴着名字的小棕瓶。一个原本属于诊室的事情，被收进了可以带回家的容器里。它不再是抽象的处方，而是一件需要记住、放好、按照说明使用的东西。What we finally received was a little brown bottle labeled with a name. Something that had belonged to a consultation was now contained in an object we could take home. A prescription was no longer abstract. It was something to remember, keep safely and use according to its instructions.

我记住这个场景，并不是因为它多么特别。反而是因为它有一种生活的烟火气。医疗系统看起来庞大而遥远，走到最后，却仍然要落在一个人的姓名、一份说明和每天怎样照顾自己上。I remember the scene not because it was extraordinary, but because it belonged so plainly to daily life. Healthcare could seem enormous and remote. At the end, it still had to settle into a person's name, a set of instructions and the small routines of looking after oneself.

看病这件事，最后不是结束在医院，而是结束在超市角落那个小小的药房窗口。The visit did not end at the hospital. It ended at a small pharmacy counter in the corner of a supermarket.
