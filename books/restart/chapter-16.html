第 16 章Chapter 16

告别德州：从小镇出发，去加州Leaving Texas: From a Small Town Toward California

德州生活终于慢慢跑顺的时候，我们又要离开了。队友有了去加州的机会，跨州搬家进入日程。之前花那么长时间认识的地方，忽然开始要用另一种眼光看：这些东西带走吗，这条路以后还会不会再走。Just as life in Texas was beginning to run smoothly, we were preparing to leave. My husband had an opportunity in California, and an interstate move entered our plans. Places I had spent so long getting to know had to be seen differently: would we take this object, and would we travel this road again?

车会开了，医生找到了，公园也去过了。生活半径一点点变大，我不再需要为每次出门从头做准备。那些曾经让人发愁的事情，终于有一些退到了背景里，变成不需要反复思考的日常。I could drive. We had found doctors and visited parks. The radius of life had slowly grown, and every outing no longer required preparation from scratch. Some things that once worried me had finally moved into the background and become ordinary routines.

收拾东西的时候，才看见空房子后来装进了多少生活。最开始买回来的用品，是为了让自己能在这里住下去。现在又要一件件判断如何处理，它们已经不只是东西，也标记着我们怎样把这里过成了自己的住处。Packing revealed how much life had filled the empty apartment. The first household items had been bought to make living here possible. Now each required another decision about what to do with it. They were more than objects. They marked how we had turned this place into our home.

离开没有让我觉得此前的摸索白费。会开车的身体、知道怎样问问题的经验、遇到事情时少一点的慌张，不会因为换州就全部清空。可新地方仍然是新地方，我也不能拿着这些，假装自己已经知道后面的路。Leaving did not make the earlier effort feel wasted. A body that could drive, experience in asking questions and a little less panic when something happened would not all disappear at a state line. Still, a new place remained new. I could not carry those skills and pretend I knew the road ahead.

我想起刚到休斯顿时那种系统重置的空白。再出发，当然仍然有不确定，只是这一次，我知道生活可以先搭起一小部分，再慢慢补齐。我们不是带着一份完整答案离开，而是带着一些重新开始的办法。I remembered the blankness of that first reset in Houston. Setting out again was still uncertain. This time, though, I knew a life could be built in small portions and completed gradually. We were leaving without a complete answer, but with some ways to begin again.

我们以为终于把德州生活跑通了。结果生活又弹出一个新任务：离开这里，重新开始。We thought we had finally worked out how life in Texas ran. Then life presented another task: leave here and start again.
