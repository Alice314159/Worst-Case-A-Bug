SSN 递归报错：到了第二学年，我跑了四趟 SSASSN Recursive Errors: Four Trips to the SSA in the Second Academic Year

SSN 的事情发生在第二学年。到那时，我已经不是刚下飞机时的状态，生活里不少流程也渐渐熟了。所以开始办这件事时，我以为准备好材料，去一趟窗口，剩下的就是等待。后来才知道，这段经历需要四趟。The SSN experience happened in the second academic year. By then, I was no longer in the state I had been in when I stepped off the plane. Many routines had become more familiar. I thought this task would mean preparing the documents, visiting a counter and waiting. It turned out to require four trips.

其中有墨水签名的问题，也遇到了服务器宕机。听到结果的时候，我常常先想，自己是不是哪里没有准备好。等确认不是所有问题都出在自己身上，那股无力反而更明显：如果出错的是别的环节，我能做的事情就更少了。There was an issue with an ink signature, and there was a server outage. When I heard a result, my first thought was often that I had failed to prepare something. Discovering that not every problem was mine made the helplessness clearer: if another part of the process had failed, there was even less I could do.

跑窗口这件事，最消耗人的不是路程本身。是每次出门前，都要重新带上一次期待。到了那里，又可能因为新的原因停下。上一次的问题解决了，并不保证这一次能够往前一步，像调用还没有返回，就又进入了下一层。The most draining part was not the journey itself. Each departure required me to carry another expectation. Once there, I might stop for a new reason. Solving the last problem did not guarantee moving a step forward this time. Before one call had returned, I seemed to have entered another level.

我借用超时、丢包和递归报错来形容它，不是因为生活真的遵循程序逻辑，而是那种感觉太熟悉：请求发出去了，结果没有回来；重新尝试，又要从开头说明一遍。系统里的等待，没有办法用着急缩短。I borrow timeouts, packet loss and recursive errors to describe it, not because life follows program logic, but because the feeling was familiar: a request had been sent and no result came back. Try again, and everything had to be explained from the beginning. Impatience could not shorten the system's waiting time.

情绪也跟着跑了几趟。最开始是愤怒，后来有些麻木，再后来，我学会只准备这一次能够准备的东西。并不是看破了什么，只是不想每回都让一个窗口占满整天的心情。My emotions made several trips too. First anger, then a degree of numbness. Eventually I learned to prepare only what I could prepare for this attempt. It was not enlightenment. I simply did not want a counter to occupy my entire day, every time.

有些号码不是你申请就有的。它要你跑窗口、补材料、等系统恢复、再跑一次。直到某一天，它终于承认：你可以被编号了。Some numbers do not arrive just because you apply. They require another counter visit, another document, a wait for the system to recover and another trip. Until, one day, it finally acknowledges that you can be assigned a number.
