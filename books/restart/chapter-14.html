第 14 章Chapter 14

德州州立公园记：蓝帽花、鳄鱼沼泽和偷香肠的犰狳Texas State Parks: Bluebonnets, Alligator Marshes and an Armadillo After the Sausages

朋友建议办一张德州州立公园年卡，我们后来在 Somerville State Park 办了。那张卡没有一次性改变生活，却为很多次出门提供了理由。德州开始从住处周围展开，不只剩下必须办事时才走的路线。A friend suggested a Texas State Parks annual pass. We eventually bought one at Somerville State Park. It did not change life all at once, but it gave us reasons to go out repeatedly. Texas began to unfold beyond our home and the routes we took only for errands.

三四月的蓝帽花，让时间有了和账单、预约不同的标记。此前我常用下一件事情的期限记日子，现在也可以用花来记。公园去了一次又一次，每次并不需要有一个很大的收获。能在外面待一会儿，本身就够了。The bluebonnets of March and April marked time differently from bills and appointments. Before, I had often measured the days by the next deadline. Now flowers could mark them too. We returned to parks again and again without needing a major achievement from each visit. Spending some time outside was enough.

Brazos Bend 的沼泽里，有荷花、荷叶和水鸟，也有鳄鱼。它们藏在水下，只露出一点眼睛的时候，危险不是一个完整呈现在面前的形状。你看不见全部，却知道它确实就在附近。The marshes at Brazos Bend held lotus flowers, leaves, waterbirds and alligators. When an alligator remained underwater with only a hint of eyes visible, danger did not present itself as a complete shape. I could not see all of it, but knew it was nearby.

自然不会因为我来散心，就把自己变得安全而温顺。它让人放松，也提醒人记得边界。走在这样的地方，我不再觉得自己必须把一切看透，才能承认眼前这片世界是真实的。Nature did not become safe and gentle simply because I had come to relax. It offered ease and also reminded me of boundaries. Walking there, I no longer felt I had to see through everything before acknowledging the reality of the world in front of me.

另一次公园露营，半夜有犰狳来偷香肠。后来讲起来很好笑，当时却是一堂具体的生活课。食物不能随意留在外面，露营地也不是一间没有墙的自家厨房。到了野外，人的习惯需要重新调整。On another camping trip, an armadillo stole sausages in the night. It was funny to tell afterward, but at the time it was a practical lesson. Food could not simply be left outside, and a campsite was not our kitchen with the walls removed. In the wild, our habits needed adjusting.

德州不再只是办手续、看病、考驾照的地方。它开始有了蓝帽花、沼泽、鳄鱼，也有一只半夜偷走香肠的犰狳。Texas was no longer only a place for paperwork, doctors and driving tests. It had bluebonnets, marshes, alligators and an armadillo that had made off with sausages in the night.
