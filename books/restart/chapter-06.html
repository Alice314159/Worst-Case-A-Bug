十三年的老凯美瑞：我们终于有了自己的腿A Thirteen-Year-Old Camry: Finally, Legs of Our Own

买车之前，我们蹲毕业学生群，留意二手车消息。看一辆车，也在看价格、车龄、使用记录和自己的预算。没有哪个选项让人完全放心，只能一点点排除问题，再决定愿意接受哪些不完美。Before buying a car, we watched graduating students' group chats for used-car listings. Looking at a car meant considering its price, age and history alongside our budget. No option felt entirely reassuring. We could only rule out problems gradually and decide which imperfections we were willing to accept.

最后进入生活的，是一辆十三年的老凯美瑞。买之前做过检查，但检查并不能让一辆旧车重新变成新车。拿到钥匙的时候，期待与忐忑是一起到来的：它终于属于我们，也带来了一整套我们还不熟悉的责任。What finally entered our life was a thirteen-year-old Camry. We had it checked before buying, but an inspection could not make an old car new. When we received the keys, anticipation and unease arrived together. It was finally ours, along with responsibilities we did not yet understand.

买后发现玻璃裂纹，我才更具体地体会到二手车的含义。有些细节只有在每天相处时才显出来。你以为购买已经结束，实际上才刚开始认识它。它的声音、状态、需要留意的地方，都要慢慢记住。Finding a crack in the glass after the purchase made the meaning of a used car more concrete. Some details only emerge through daily use. You think the purchase is finished, but getting to know the car has just begun. Its sounds, condition and points that need attention all have to be learned.

后来一次爆胎，又让我们看到了右后轮附近曾经受撞、补漆的痕迹。那段过去不是我们经历的，却已经留在车上。二手车不是一辆打了折的新车，而是一段已经运行过很久的历史。Later, a flat tire revealed signs of an old collision and repainting near the right rear wheel. We had not lived through that history, but it remained in the car. A used car is not a discounted new one. It is a history that has already been running for years.

可它也确实把生活带远了。原本需要先查公交的地方，终于能按自己的时间出发。行李和买回来的东西有了安放的地方，公园与更远的城市也不再只是地图上的名字。担心没有消失，行动能力却真切地增加了。Yet it really did carry our life farther. Places that once required a look at the bus timetable could now be reached on our own schedule. Luggage and shopping had somewhere to go. Parks and distant cities were no longer only names on a map. The worries remained, but our ability to act had undeniably grown.

我们买到的不是一辆完美的车，而是一台带着过去的老系统。但它能启动，能上路，能带我们去更远的地方。We had not bought a perfect car. We had bought an old system carrying its past. But it could start, take to the road and bring us farther than before.
