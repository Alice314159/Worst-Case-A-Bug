车险、Title 和 Registration：自由也要先绑定系统Insurance, Title and Registration: Even Freedom Needed Paperwork

有了车，不等于已经可以安心开。钥匙在手里，保险、产权转让和车辆注册还在等着。一件期待了很久的事完成之后，后面常常不是空白，而是下一张清单。Having a car did not mean we could drive without concern. The keys were in our hands, but insurance, a title transfer and registration still awaited us. Completing something we had wanted for a long time did not lead to an empty space. It led to the next checklist.

新手司机的保险报价让我心疼。各种选项摆在面前，每一个看起来都认识，组合在一起又不知道该怎样比较。花多少钱、承担多少风险、适不适合自己的驾驶频率，都需要重新想一遍。Insurance quotes for a new driver hurt. The individual options looked recognizable, yet together I did not know how to compare them. How much to spend, how much risk to carry and what suited how often we drove all required thought.

最后，我们选择了当时适合自己的保险安排。做决定的过程并没有让我突然成为专家，只是把几个最重要的问题逐渐问清楚：自己平时怎么用车，哪些部分需要覆盖，什么样的支出能够承受。Eventually we chose an arrangement that fit us at the time. The decision did not suddenly make me an expert. It meant gradually clarifying the important questions: how we used the car, what needed covering and what we could afford.

Title 和 Registration 又是另一组需要学习的词。车不只是一件买回来的东西，它还必须和人、地址、签名以及记录对应起来。到了县税务办公室，拿材料、排队、缴费、等待，才知道所谓拥有，也有一层纸面上的过程。Title and registration were another set of words to learn. The car was not simply something we had bought. It also had to correspond to a person, an address, a signature and a record. At the county tax office, documents, queues, payments and waiting showed me that ownership had a paper process too.

我开始不再把这些手续完全看成出发前多余的障碍。它们当然烦，也会消耗时间。但生活在这里，车辆的归属与使用，不会因为双方交过钥匙就自动被所有系统承认。自己认定的完成，与记录里的完成，中间还有路要走。I began to see these procedures as more than unnecessary obstacles before departure. They were frustrating and time-consuming, of course. But here, a car's ownership and use did not become recognized by every system simply because two people exchanged keys. Completion in our minds and completion in the records were still some distance apart.

自由不是钥匙一拧就来了。它还需要保险、注册、地址、签名和一张张被系统承认的纸。Freedom did not arrive with a turn of the key. It also needed insurance, registration, an address, signatures and one sheet of paper after another that the system would accept.
