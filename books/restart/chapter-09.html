Houston, we have a problem：带伤回来的火箭Houston, We Have a Problem: The Rocket That Came Back Scarred

冬季寒假，我们开车去休斯顿航天中心。老公开车，我坐在副驾驶。对于已经习惯围着小镇安排日子的我们，这也是一次把生活半径向外推的出行。目的地不再是必须办完的一件事，而是一个想亲眼看看的地方。During the winter break, we drove to Space Center Houston. My husband drove; I sat in the passenger seat. For us, accustomed to arranging our days around a small town, it was another extension of our radius. The destination was not an obligation to complete, but somewhere we wanted to see for ourselves.

到了那里，那句熟悉的 Houston, we have a problem，忽然不再只是影视与故事里的声音。一个用来承认问题的句子，也可以和认真解决问题的人、设备与历史放在一起。说出问题，未必就是失败的开头。There, the familiar words Houston, we have a problem no longer belonged only to films and stories. A sentence that admitted a problem could sit alongside the people, equipment and history of working seriously to solve it. Naming a problem did not have to be the beginning of failure.

我记得猎鹰 9 号一级火箭上的灼烧痕迹。它不是想象里光洁、无损的样子。那些深色的痕迹很明显，让人无法忽略它确实经历过怎样的过程。可重复使用这个概念，到了实物面前，才有了重量。I remember the scorch marks on the Falcon 9 first stage. It was not the smooth, untouched object I had imagined. The dark marks were unmistakable, impossible to ignore as evidence of what it had been through. Reusability acquired weight in front of the actual thing.

我也摸到了月球岩石。手指与那块石头接触时，有一种不真实感。平时习惯把很远的事情想成屏幕上的知识，忽然它就来到触手可及的地方，而自己仍然只是一个在生活里努力弄懂各种小事的人。I also touched a moon rock. The contact felt unreal. I was used to knowing distant things through screens. Suddenly one of them was within reach, while I remained an ordinary person trying to understand all the small matters of daily life.

那天真正留在我心里的，还是那枚带着痕迹回来的火箭。我总盼望一切先修好，自己先准备充分，再继续下一步。可眼前的东西告诉我，可靠也可以是经历过问题之后，仍然拥有返回和再次出发的能力。What stayed with me was the rocket that had returned bearing marks. I was always hoping to repair everything and become fully prepared before taking the next step. Yet the object in front of me suggested another kind of reliability: going through trouble and still being able to return and set out again.

那枚熏黑的火箭让我第一次觉得，带着痕迹继续运行，不是一种失败。它甚至可以是一种荣耀。That blackened rocket made me feel, for the first time, that continuing with visible traces was not a failure. It could even be a kind of honor.
