第一根生活样柱：从酒店、空房子和第一顿饭开始The First Supports of Daily Life: A Hotel, an Empty Apartment and a Meal

刚来的几天，生活先暂存在酒店里。箱子打开，取出需要的东西，再塞回去。第一顿汉堡，人均二十刀，让我对这里的价格有了很直接的认识。换算还在脑子里自动进行，一顿普通的饭，也要花一点时间接受。For the first few days, our life was temporarily stored in a hotel. We opened our suitcases, took out what we needed and put things back. Our first burger meal cost twenty dollars per person and gave me a direct introduction to prices here. My mind was still converting everything; even an ordinary meal required adjustment.

真正住进公寓，反而比住酒店更清楚地看见了从零开始。屋子是空的，没有床、桌子和椅子。门关上以后，行李似乎成了最完整的家具。房子已经租到了，家却还没有长出来。Moving into the apartment made starting from zero clearer than the hotel had. The rooms were empty. There was no bed, table or chair. Once the door closed, our luggage seemed to be the most complete piece of furniture. We had rented a place, but a home had not yet grown inside it.

睡在地板上的日子，把很多抽象的选择变得很具体。不是先想装修成什么样，而是先有地方睡；不是研究厨房的风格，而是先能做一顿饭。从亚马逊和超市一点点买回来，东西到了一件，日子就多一处可以落脚。Sleeping on the floor made many abstract choices concrete. Before deciding how a room should look, we needed somewhere to sleep. Before thinking about a kitchen's style, we needed to cook a meal. We bought things little by little from Amazon and the supermarket. With each arrival, daily life gained another place to stand.

在 HEB 买菜，我又一次觉得自己像个文盲。并非所有字都不认识，而是熟悉的食物换了包装、单位和摆放方式。磅、加仑、华氏度、英里，生活里那些本来不需要翻译的数值，现在都有了额外的一道计算。At HEB, I felt illiterate again. It was not that I could read none of the words. Familiar food had changed its packaging, units and location on the shelves. Pounds, gallons, Fahrenheit and miles added an extra calculation to numbers that once needed no translation.

逛一次超市，并不只是把购物清单买齐。也是在重新学习怎样判断够不够、贵不贵、能不能放进自己的厨房。许多小时就花在这种事上。它们很难拿来汇报，却是生活开始运转真正需要的部分。A supermarket trip was more than completing a shopping list. It was learning again how to judge enough, expensive and suitable for our kitchen. Many hours went into these tasks. They were difficult to put in a progress report, but they were what actually allowed life to begin working.

后来再看那间空房子，我记住的已经不只是空。生活不是一下子安顿下来的。它是从一张床、一口锅、一袋米、一盏灯开始，慢慢长出边界的。When I looked back at that apartment, emptiness was no longer all I remembered. A life does not settle into place at once. It begins with a bed, a pot, a bag of rice and a lamp, slowly growing its own edges.
