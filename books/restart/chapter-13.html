第 13 章Chapter 13

Whatever 医生和 PCP 法则The Whatever Doctor and the Importance of a PCP

后来，好不容易约到一个几天后的号，我带着终于能被接住的期待去看医生。时间上比排到明年好得多，体验却没有因此一起变好。坐到诊室里，只完成了第一步。Later, I finally found an appointment only a few days away. I went with the expectation of being received at last. The date was much better than next year, but the experience did not improve along with it. Sitting in the examination room was only the first step.

那位医生的态度让我觉得敷衍。关于检查，得到的意思近乎是，想做也行，不想做也行。我需要的是解释与判断，却感觉自己被留在一张菜单前，不知道该怎样点下去。The doctor's manner felt dismissive. The message about tests seemed to be that having them was fine, and not having them was fine too. I needed explanation and judgment. Instead I felt as though I had been left in front of a menu without knowing how to order.

我并不期待医生替我承包全部决定。但在一个自己不熟悉的领域里，知道有哪些选择、为什么考虑它们，与被要求直接选一个，是很不一样的事。那次经历以后，我才更清楚自己希望从医疗关系里得到什么。I did not expect a doctor to take over every decision. But in an unfamiliar field, understanding the options and the reasons for considering them is very different from being asked to pick one immediately. After that visit, I understood more clearly what I wanted from a relationship of care.

后来，我逐渐理解了 PCP，也就是日常照护中家庭医生这一入口的意义。对我而言，它不只是在生病时临时找到一个人，而是让身体的情况能够被连续认识，不必每次都从陌生的新病人开始。Gradually I came to understand the value of a PCP, a primary care physician. For me, it was not only about finding someone temporarily when I was ill. It meant allowing my health to become familiar to someone over time, rather than beginning every visit as an unknown new patient.

从 New Patient 到 Established Patient，字面上只是一个类别的变化，实际却让我更容易安排后面的预约。这也让我开始想，在健康的时候把基础关系建起来，遇到不适合自己的医生时再寻找别的选择，都是照顾自己的部分。Moving from new patient to established patient looked like a small change of category, but it made arranging later appointments easier for me. Building that basic relationship while healthy, and looking for another doctor when the fit was poor, became parts of looking after myself.

在北美医疗系统里，家庭医生不只是一个医生。他更像我在陌生系统里提前埋下的一个求助入口。A primary care doctor was more than a doctor to me. It was a place to ask for help that I had established in advance, inside an unfamiliar system.
