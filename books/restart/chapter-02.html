F2：一个存在感很强、权限感很弱的身份F2: A Visible Status with Very Little Access

到美国之后，我很快学会了用 F2 介绍自己的身份。两个字符，说起来很短，却会在许多场合先于我本人出现。它说明我为什么在这里，也常常决定一件事要从哪里开始。Soon after arriving in America, I learned to introduce my status as F2. Two characters, quick to say, yet in many situations they arrived before I did. They explained why I was here and often determined where a task could begin.

队友有课程、有学校的安排，生活很快被一张张时间表接住。我的时间看起来更自由，却不容易找到落点。学校资源主要围绕学生展开，而我不是那个默认被考虑的使用者。有些地方可以去，有些事需要再问一句，有些入口从一开始就不朝我打开。My husband had classes and university arrangements. His life was quickly caught by one timetable after another. My time appeared freer, yet I struggled to find somewhere for it to land. University resources largely revolved around students; I was not their default user. Some places were open to me, some required another question, and some entrances did not face me at all.

不能工作的限制，让原本习惯用工作安排一天的我失去了一种熟悉的坐标。没有人每天催我出门，也没有一个进度表说明自己到了哪里。别人问起最近在做什么，我得先想一想，生活里的这些琐碎，能不能算一个回答。The restriction on working removed a familiar coordinate from my days. Nobody expected me to leave home each morning, and no progress chart told me how far I had come. When someone asked what I had been doing, I had to consider whether all these small matters of daily life counted as an answer.

这不是完全没有自由。恰恰因为时间似乎很多，那种停在外围的感觉才更难解释。我可以安排今天，却不能照搬过去那套证明自己正在向前的方法。合法存在与拥有主入口，并不是同一回事。This was not an absence of all freedom. The apparent abundance of time made the feeling of remaining on the edge harder to explain. I could arrange my day, but I could not reuse the old ways of proving that I was moving forward. Being legally present and having a main entrance were not the same thing.

我用了不少时间，才把身份的边界与自己的边界分开一点。系统里没有为我预留的位置，不表示我只能等着。买菜、学语言、练车、认识新的地方，这些事开始慢慢填进日子。它们没有统一的名称，却确实在改变生活。It took time to separate the boundaries of my status from the boundaries of myself. A system's failure to reserve a place for me did not mean I could only wait. Grocery shopping, language learning, driving practice and getting to know new places began to fill my days. They had no single name, but they were changing my life.

我最开始以为，F2 是一种附属身份。后来才慢慢发现，身份可以是系统给你的标签，但生活必须由自己重新编写。At first I thought F2 was a supporting identity. Gradually I understood that a system could give me a label, but I would have to write my own life again.
