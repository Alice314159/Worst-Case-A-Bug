三次路考：不是我不会开，是系统没看见Three Driving Tests: Doing It Was Not Enough to Be Seen

路考一共去了三次。后来回头讲，好像只需要一个数字，就能把那段经历收起来。但当时每一次，都有重新准备、到场、等待和带着结果回去的过程。没有通过的那天，下一次并不会自动出现在眼前。I went for the driving test three times. Looking back, a single number seems enough to contain the experience. At the time, each attempt meant preparing again, arriving, waiting and taking the result home. On a day I did not pass, the next opportunity did not appear automatically.

第一次的问题出在车灯、刹车灯上。车还没真正开上考试的路，就先被挡在外面。我准备的是驾驶，最先接受检查的却是车。那种还没开始就结束的落差，让人一时不知道该把失望放在哪里。The first problem involved the lights and brake lights. Before the car had properly entered the test route, we were stopped. I had prepared to demonstrate driving; the car was examined first. The disappointment of finishing before I had started was difficult to know what to do with.

第二次，问题终于到了我身上。Stop Sign 前停得不够明显，左右观察也不够明显。自己觉得已经注意到了，不等于考官能从我的动作里确认这一点。安全不能只发生在心里，还需要被外部看见。The second time, the problem finally reached me. My stop at a stop sign was not clear enough, and neither were my checks to the left and right. Feeling that I had paid attention did not mean the examiner could verify it from my movements. Safety had to exist outside my mind, where someone else could see it.

这让我重新理解了路考。它不只是问我会不会操纵一辆车，也在问，我能不能用明确、稳定的动作告诉别人：我注意到了这里的风险，知道下一步要怎样做。模糊的习惯，在考场里会变成很具体的问题。That changed my understanding of the test. It was not only asking whether I could operate a car. It was asking whether I could communicate through clear, steady actions: I had noticed the risk and knew what to do next. An imprecise habit became a very specific problem in the test.

第三次终于通过。前两次并没有因此变得值得，也不需要硬说每次受挫都有特别意义。只是我把车况检查、停车和观察，一项项做得更清楚了。那张通过的结果，是很多具体动作累积出来的。I passed on the third attempt. That did not make the first two feel worthwhile, and I did not need to insist that every setback carried a special meaning. I had simply made the vehicle checks, stops and observations clearer, one by one. Passing was the accumulation of those concrete actions.

很多时候，不是你做了就够了。你还要让系统看见你做了。Often, doing something is not enough. You also have to let the system see that you have done it.
