cleanCut

同一句录了五遍，它把五个版本并排放好，留一个就行。剪掉的那些停顿和口癖，其实也是说话的一部分——只是不必都留给听众。 Say a line five times and it lays the five takes side by side; keep one. The pauses and fillers it removes are part of speaking too — they just don't all need an audience.

先想办法证伪，再谈信心。Try to break it first.

只写方法，不放收益数字，也不构成建议。写下来是为了提醒自己：确定感往往比错误更贵。 Method only — no performance numbers, no advice. I write it down to remind myself that certainty costs more than being wrong.

01

先承认市场会换性格The market changes personality

用隐马尔可夫把行情切成几个状态，不是为了预测下一根 K 线，而是判断「现在这套逻辑还适用吗」。状态判错的代价，往往比信号弱一点更贵。 A hidden Markov model cuts the tape into regimes — not to predict the next candle, but to ask whether the logic I'm running still applies. Misreading the regime usually costs more than a weak signal does.

02

动量与回归之间那条线Where momentum becomes reversal

同一个信号，换个时间尺度就换个方向。短周期像回归，长周期像动量，中间那段最贵。大部分工作就是反复量这条线在哪。 The same signal flips sign when the horizon changes: short looks like reversal, long looks like momentum, and the middle is expensive. Most of the work is measuring where that line sits.

03

一个想法怎么走完流程How an idea gets tested

假设 → 构造 → 分层检验 → 加上成本和容量 → 小仓位跟一段。任何一步说不通就归档。 Hypothesis, construction, quantile test, costs and capacity, then small-size tracking. If any step doesn't hold up, it gets archived.

归档的想法比留下的多得多，我现在觉得这挺好——大部分工作，本来就是学着放手。 Far more ideas get archived than kept, and I've come to like that — most of the work is learning to let go.

英文小诗Poems

I

Rings of Time and Light

Muir Woods · 2026

Sunlight falls
through a thousand years of leaves,
waving a soft mist in the air.

I lift my head
and see the sharp edge of light,
cutting through silence.

In the slow breeze of time,
the redwood stands—
its trunk laid open,
rings unfolding
like quiet stories.

Eighty—
the world begins to count.
One hundred and five—
paper is born in the East,
hands shaping words
from pulp and fiber.

And still,
this tree was only learning
how to reach the sky.

Now their paths meet—
ink and green,
salt and root.
I trace the lines in the wood,
feeling the pulse of centuries.

Time does not pass.
It rests,
softly kept
by the forest.

II

Mooncake Waiting

Mid-Autumn · 2026

The moon was full, but I was not home.
Mid-Autumn came and went
through phone calls
that ended too soon,
through promises
that stretched too far.

You had already saved one for me—
a mooncake, round and patient,
resting quietly on the table.

From that night on, it stayed there,
watching seasons shift,
counting footsteps that never came.

When I finally returned,
the air had turned cool.
You were sitting by the door,
as if time had decided to wait with you.

You did not ask why.
You only smiled,
and placed the mooncake
into my hands.

It felt lighter—
as though something inside
had already faded.

Only then I saw
the soft bloom of mold,
spreading like a forgotten story
across its surface.

I wanted to speak,
but the silence
knew me better.

So I held it there—
that once-perfect circle—
and understood
how long love can wait without being seen.
