listen, repeat, learn

听懂 → 开口说 → 存下来复习 → 再往上走一层。图标里那两个气泡就是这个循环：语言不是背出来的，是用出来的。 Listen, say it out loud, save it, come back stronger. The two bubbles in the icon are that loop — a language gets used into place, not memorised.
