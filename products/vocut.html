voCut

录音 + 剪辑，一个窗口搞定Record and cut in one window

从录音、降噪、转写到剪辑导出，一套流程处理播客素材。可按文字定位片段、复核重复语句建议，也能重录不满意的段落；每个删除决定都由你确认。 Take podcast audio from recording and denoising through transcription and editing. Find passages by text, review repeated-phrase suggestions, and rerecord sections you want to improve. You approve every cut.

macOSWindows自用中In daily use
仓库暂未公开Repository not yet public

录一段播客，往往还要检查音质、删掉说错和重复的部分、补录不满意的句子，再导出成片。voCut 把这些步骤连在同一个工作台里，让转写和算法提供线索，由你试听、判断并完成剪辑。Finishing a podcast often means checking audio quality, removing mistakes and repeats, rerecording a sentence, then exporting the final take. voCut brings these steps into one workspace: transcription and analysis help surface candidates while you listen, decide and edit.

01

录制或导入Record or import

在浏览器录音，或导入已有音频／视频。实时查看波形与响度、峰值、削波和噪声等质量提示。Record in the browser or import existing audio/video. See the waveform and quality indicators for loudness, peaks, clipping and noise.

02

增强并对照试听Enhance and compare

选择不同强度的谱减法降噪，保留独立版本，随时对照原音与处理结果。Choose a spectral-subtraction denoising strength, keep separate versions, and compare the result with the original.

03

跟着转写复核Review with a transcript

faster-whisper 在本机转写并标注时间。点击或拖选文字定位片段；相邻重复等剪辑建议会等待人工接受或拒绝。faster-whisper transcribes locally with timestamps. Select words to locate a passage; suggestions such as repeated phrases wait for your approval or rejection.

04

精确剪辑与重录Edit and retake

按文字或时间范围标记删除，试听候选区间，也能重录不满意的片段并替换，然后导出 WAV 成片及 TXT／SRT。Mark passages by text or time, audition candidate cuts, and rerecord a passage to replace it. Export a WAV edit plus TXT/SRT transcripts.

当前是本机原型：转写与音频处理在本地服务中运行；剪辑建议需要人工确认。尚未实现实时语义识别或自动口误判断。This is a local prototype: transcription and audio processing run in the local service, and suggested cuts require human review. Real-time semantic detection and automatic mistake detection are not implemented.
