PhotoFlow

快速浏览，安心挑片Browse fast. Cull with confidence.

为 macOS 打造的本地照片挑选工具。快速浏览 JPEG、HEIC 和相机 RAW，读取拍摄信息，通过搜索、筛选和智能分组找到想要的照片，再用星级、颜色与旗标完成挑片。原片保持不变，标记保存在本机。 A local photo culling tool for macOS. Browse JPEG, HEIC and camera RAW files, inspect capture details, find shots with search, filters and smart groups, then cull with stars, color labels and flags. Originals stay untouched; marks are stored locally.

面对一整个文件夹的照片，不必逐个打开、靠记忆挑选。PhotoFlow 把浏览、筛选和标记放进同一个工作流，帮你更快整理出值得保留的照片。When a folder is full of photos, there is no need to open each file or keep your picks in your head. PhotoFlow brings browsing, filtering and rating into one workflow so you can find the keepers faster.

01

浏览原片Browse originals

网格与放大预览，支持 JPEG、HEIC、TIFF、WebP、PNG 及 CR3、ARW、NEF、RAF、DNG 等相机 RAW 格式。Move between grid and loupe views. Supports JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP, PNG and camera RAW formats including CR3, ARW, NEF, RAF and DNG.

02

快速找到照片Find the right shots

按文件名、日期、相机信息和内容搜索；叠加镜头、曝光参数、星级、颜色与旗标筛选。Search by filename, date, camera details or image content; combine filters for lens, exposure, ratings, colors and flags.

03

让挑片有条理Cull with a clear system

用星级、颜色和留用／排除旗标批量标记。撤销、重做和快捷键让连续挑片更流畅。Apply star ratings, color labels and pick/reject flags to selections. Undo, redo and keyboard shortcuts keep culling moving.

04

本地保存，原片不动Local by design

原始照片不会被修改。标记、收藏和缓存保存在这台 Mac 上；可选择导出副本。Original photos are never changed. Marks, collections and cache stay on this Mac, and you can export copies when needed.
