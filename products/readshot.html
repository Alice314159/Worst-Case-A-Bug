READING / LANGUAGE / MEMORY

ReadShot

Capture · Understand · Remember

拍下来，读懂它，再记住它。Capture it. Understand it. Remember it.

从纸页上的一道标记开始，让单词带着原句、页码和阅读时的记忆，回到下一次复习里。Begin with a mark on a page. Bring a word back to review with its sentence, page and the memory of where you found it.

当前提供 Android 测试版；产品蓝图与目标 V1 功能在下方单独说明。An Android beta is available. The product vision and target V1 scope are described separately below.

先让一次阅读，真正走完一圈。First, complete one learning loop.

第一版围绕采集、确认、理解与复习展开。以下为目标能力，不表示当前测试包已经全部实现。The first target release focuses on capture, confirmation, understanding and review. These are planned capabilities, not a claim that every item exists in the current beta.

  1. 01

    采集标记Capture marks

    拍照、导入与 OCR，找到被标记的词。Capture or import a page; detect marked words with OCR.

  2. 02

    人工确认Confirm the result

    在原图上核对位置，增删误识别的单词。Check locations on the original image and correct recognition errors.

  3. 03

    保留语境Keep the context

    词条与原句、页码、书籍一起保存。Save the word with its sentence, page and source book.

  4. 04

    安排复习Return to review

    按掌握程度进入每日复习，回到原文理解。Schedule daily review by recall and return to the original context.

确认，是学习闭环的一部分。Confirmation belongs in the loop.

标记识别之后先让用户确认，再写入单词本，避免误识别直接污染学习资料。Review recognition results before adding them to the wordbook, so detection errors do not silently enter saved study material.

01

智能拍照与标记识别Capture and mark recognition

+

相机、相册与批量导入；识别高亮、下划线和圈词，在原图标出位置，并允许手动增删。Camera, photo library and batch import; detect highlights, underlines and circles, show locations on the image and allow manual corrections.

02

智能单词本A wordbook with context

+

词形还原、去重、音标与发音、中英文释义、词性、CEFR 等级、原文例句与来源书籍；按书籍、日期、标签和掌握程度筛选。Lemmatization, deduplication, pronunciation, bilingual definitions, parts of speech, CEFR levels, source sentences and books; filter by book, date, tag and mastery.

03

智能复习Review that returns on time

+

闪卡、认识／模糊／忘记评分、每日待复习列表、间隔重复调度与掌握状态；FSRS 作为后续调度升级方向。Flashcards, recall ratings, daily due lists, spaced scheduling and mastery states; FSRS is a possible later scheduling upgrade.

04

阅读来源管理Reading sources

+

建立书籍、课程或阅读项目，记录照片、页码、原文和提取日期；同一单词可关联多个来源。Create books, courses or reading projects; retain photos, page numbers, source text and capture dates. A word can link to multiple sources.

05

基础体验与数据保障Essential experience and data care

+

基础设置、离线访问已保存单词、数据导出与本地备份、隐私说明、识别失败重试，以及图片删除。Settings, offline access to saved words, export and local backup, privacy information, recognition retries and image deletion.

长期蓝图LONG-TERM VISION

八个方向，不必同时出发。Eight directions, one step at a time.

+

按学习闭环逐步扩展，优先验证持续使用，再增加复杂能力。Expand from the learning loop. Validate sustained use before adding complexity.

界面与信息结构 / 概念INTERFACE & INFORMATION / CONCEPT

四个底部入口，把扫描留在手边。Four destinations, with capture close at hand.

Home、Library、Review、Insights 分担日常任务；Scan 作为突出的扫描入口。AI 导师从词条与阅读详情进入，不必一开始独占一个导航位置。Home, Library, Review and Insights organize daily tasks, with Scan as a prominent capture action. A tutor can begin within word and reading details without requiring its own tab.

差异化方向WHAT MAKES IT DISTINCT

记住的不只是一个释义。Remember more than a definition.

+
V1

原文语境记忆Memory in context

保留句子、页码与当时的标记。复习时先从原句猜词义，而不只是记住“inevitable = 不可避免的”。Keep the sentence, page and original mark. Recall meaning through the source context rather than only a translated definition.

V1.5

AI 个性化英语导师A personalized English tutor

围绕当前语境解释词义，并规划适合用户水平的例句、选择题、填空题与写作练习。Explain meaning within context and plan level-appropriate examples, multiple-choice questions, cloze exercises and writing practice.

V2

单词关系网络A network of words

把同义、反义、词根词缀、搭配和主题关系连起来，例如 revenge、vengeance、retaliation。Connect synonyms, antonyms, roots, affixes, collocations and themes, such as revenge, vengeance and retaliation.

V2

AI 听力与播客Personal listening material

用新增词汇和阅读内容生成计划约 3–5 分钟的原创英语材料，加入词汇复现、解释与练习，并支持调节语速。Generate original English listening material from new words and reading, planned at about 3–5 minutes, with repetition, explanation, exercises and adjustable speed.

V3

阅读时间胶囊Reading time capsules

把某一天的书、句子与笔记留到未来再打开，让单词本逐渐成为阅读记忆库。Return later to a day’s books, marked sentences and notes, allowing a wordbook to become a lasting reading memory.

版本演进 / 建议排期VERSION ROADMAP / INDICATIVE TIMING

先跑通，再长大。Make it work, then let it grow.

以下时间按项目启动后的月份估算，不是发布日期承诺；尚未设定具体起始日期。Timing is estimated in months from a project start, not a release commitment. No calendar start date has been set.

    核心学习闭环Core learning loop

    真实标记识别、单词本、原文语境、闪卡、阅读来源与数据导出。Mark recognition, wordbook, source context, flashcards, reading sources and export.

    验证用户愿意持续使用Validate sustained use
  2. V1.5第 3–4 个月Months 3–4

    AI 学习增强AI-assisted learning

    语境解释、自动练习、个性化难度与批量识别优化。Context explanations, generated exercises, personalized difficulty and improved batch recognition.

    提升学习效果和留存Improve learning and retention
  3. V2.0第 5–8 个月Months 5–8

    多格式与多模态More formats and modalities

    PDF 与电子书导入、网页剪藏、词汇关系、听力材料与 Anki 导出。PDF and e-book import, web clipping, word relationships, listening material and Anki export.

    扩展使用场景Broaden reading contexts
  4. V3.0第 9–12 个月Months 9–12

    长期阅读记忆Long-term reading memory
