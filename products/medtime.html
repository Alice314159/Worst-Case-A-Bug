MedTime

medicine, on time

给家里人做的：几种药、几点吃、还剩多少，都替他们记着。提醒要足够明显，操作要足够少。 Built for family: which pills, what time, how many left — all remembered for them. Loud enough to notice, simple enough to use.

Android提醒Reminders家人Family
功能与页面预览Features & screen previews

围绕“按时服用”设计：管理药品、每日计划和剩余库存，用 Android 闹钟与通知提醒服药，并可语音播报。首页汇总当天待服、已完成和逾期时段，也提示低库存；药盒照片可辅助录入药品信息。Designed around taking medication on time: manage medicines, daily schedules and remaining supply, then use Android alarms, notifications and voice prompts for reminders. The home screen summarizes upcoming, completed and overdue doses and flags low stock; a pill-box photo can help enter medication details.
