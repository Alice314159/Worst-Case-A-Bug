attentionStudio

注意力是视频真正的来源Attention is where video actually comes from

一条视频最终是由注意力生成的——所以它管理的不是文件，是 prompt 本身：按分镜拆开，每镜一条，版本并排比，生成结果挂在对应版本下，好用的存成模板。 A video is ultimately generated out of attention — so this manages prompts, not files: broken down shot by shot, revisions side by side with their outputs attached, the good ones saved as templates.

功能与页面预览Features & screen previews

围绕 AI 视频项目管理人物、空间、镜头与分集；Prompt 按项目设定、角色、空间、镜头和模型覆盖逐层合成，并为每次修改保存版本快照。可以对照生成版本及对应成片引用、导入拆镜表、筛选待复核素材，并追踪分集制作进度。Organize characters, spaces, shots and episodes for AI video projects. Prompts are composed from project, character, space, shot and model layers, with a snapshot for each revision. Compare generated versions and their episode references, import shot lists, review assets and track episode progress.
