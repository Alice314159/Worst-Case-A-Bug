VESPER / ETF MONITOR

把变化记下来，再决定要不要行动。Notice the change before acting on it.

研究之外，还需要一张能反复查看的观察表。ETF Monitor 把行情更新、技术面变化和风险提示串起来，让值得复核的地方先浮出来。Alongside research, there is the need for a repeatable monitoring routine. ETF Monitor brings updates, technical changes and risk flags together so there is a clearer place to begin a review.

行情维护Price updates

技术面预警Technical flags

观察 5 日与 20 日均线、均线方向、近期高点回撤，支持定时扫描与终端报告。Track 5/20-day moving averages, their direction and drawdown from recent highs, with scheduled scans and terminal reports.

因子与状态模块Factor and regime modules

后端包含溢价、份额、情绪、拥挤等因子模块，以及同类资产分组标准化和市场状态模块。Backend modules cover premium, shares, sentiment and crowding factors, with asset-bucket normalization and market-regime components.

METHOD / 公式与口径FORMULAS & CONVENTIONS

把计算的来路写清。Make the calculation explicit.

以下区分源码对应定义与通用解释。窗口、频率和参数以具体实验配置为准；所有信号只使用当时已可获得的数据。Implementation-linked definitions and general explanations are distinguished below. Windows, frequencies and parameters follow each experiment; signals must use only information available at the time.

01

均线与方向Moving averages and direction

MAₖ(t) = (Pₜ + Pₜ₋₁ + … + Pₜ₋ₖ₊₁) / k ΔMAₖ(t) = MAₖ(t) − MAₖ(t−1)

k 取 5 或 20；ΔMA 表示单期方向，阈值与连续确认天数属于扫描配置。缺少足够历史时不应填零。k is 5 or 20. ΔMA is a one-period change; thresholds and confirmation periods are scanner settings. Insufficient history should not be filled with zero.

02

近期高点回撤与溢价Drawdown and premium

Hₜ,ₗ = max(Pₜ₋ₗ₊₁,…,Pₜ); DDₜ = Pₜ / Hₜ,ₗ − 1 Premiumₜ = MarketPriceₜ / NAVₜ − 1

DD 使用负值口径，L 为高点回看窗口。溢价为通用定义；市场价和单位净值必须匹配日期、币种及可获得时间，不能把昨日净值当实时净值。DD is non-positive; L is the high-water lookback. Premium is a general definition. Match market price and per-unit NAV by date, currency and availability time.

RESEARCH COMPANION

预警之后，还需要一次判断。A flag still needs interpretation.

阅读项目时，先沿着输入与输出走一遍，再展开值得追问的细节。以下是验证思路，不表示这些检查已全部完成。Follow the inputs and outputs, then explore the questions below. These are validation approaches, not claims that every check has been completed.

01 / INPUT

从什么开始What goes in

已核对身份的 ETF 日线、均线窗口、近期高点窗口和预警阈值。Verified ETF bars, moving-average windows, recent-high lookbacks and alert thresholds.

02 / OUTPUT

带着什么离开What comes out

带日期的指标快照、命中条件与待复核项，不将预警等同于交易指令。Dated indicator snapshots, triggered rules and review items; flags are not trade instructions.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01数据没有更新，还是行情没有变化？Unchanged price or stale observation?+

先核实最后交易日、停牌状态和采集结果。缺失记录单列处理，避免缓存里的旧值反复生成预警。Check the last trading date, suspensions and collection results. Handle missing observations separately so stale cache values do not repeatedly trigger flags.

02多个条件同时命中，怎样阅读？How should overlapping flags be read?+

在同一快照里保留均线方向、价格位置与回撤窗口。先看条件是否来自同一日期，再分析它们是否表达了同一种变化。Keep average direction, price position and drawdown lookback in one snapshot. Verify matching dates before interpreting whether flags describe the same change.

预警是待检查的线索，不代表交易已执行。Alerts are prompts for review, not evidence of an executed trade.
