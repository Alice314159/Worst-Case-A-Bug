模型给出一个答案，研究还要继续问。A model output is another question.

这些项目留下了不同阶段的尝试：下载数据、构造指标、训练模型，再把预测放回时间顺序里检查。相比一个好看的拟合结果，我更关心训练和验证之间有没有被忽略的缝隙。These projects preserve several stages of experimentation: downloading data, building indicators, training models and reviewing predictions in time order. The boundary between training and evaluation matters as much as the fitted result.

01

标准化与时间边界Scaling and time boundaries

zₜ,ⱼ = (xₜ,ⱼ − μtrain,ⱼ) / σtrain,ⱼ

均值与标准差仅在训练集估计，并固定用于验证、测试。标签跨越预测区间时，应留出间隔，避免训练标签使用测试区间信息；零方差特征单独处理。Fit mean and standard deviation only on training data and reuse them for validation/test. Leave a gap where label horizons overlap the next split; handle constant features separately.

02

回归与方向分类损失Regression and direction losses

MSE = (1/N) Σᵢ (ŷᵢ − yᵢ)² BCE = −(1/N) Σᵢ [yᵢ ln pᵢ + (1−yᵢ) ln(1−pᵢ)]

通用评估定义，不表示所有实验使用同一训练损失。MSE 面向数值目标，BCE 面向二元方向标签；p 为上涨概率，计算对数时须做数值保护。误差应在目标还原后的同一尺度比较。General evaluation definitions, not a claim of a shared training loss. MSE evaluates numeric targets; BCE evaluates binary labels with probability p. Protect logarithms numerically and compare errors on a consistent, inverse-transformed target scale.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01复杂模型真的优于简单基线吗？Does complexity improve on a baseline?+

在相同区间、目标和尺度下，与持平预测或简单历史规则比较。先确认基线与模型使用相同的可用信息，再讨论改进。Compare against persistence or simple historical rules on the same interval, target and scale. Match information availability before interpreting improvements.

02预测准确，就能得到好的回测吗？Does predictive accuracy imply a good backtest?+

检查信号阈值、执行滞后、换手和成本。价格误差、方向命中与策略收益衡量的是不同问题，需要分别报告。Inspect thresholds, execution lags, turnover and costs. Price error, directional accuracy and strategy return answer different questions and should be reported separately.
