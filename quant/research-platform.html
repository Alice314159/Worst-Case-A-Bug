VESPER / RESEARCH SERVICES

把一条研究流程，拆成能接上的小块。Give each part of the research a place.

当下载、指标和回测逐渐长大，项目之间如何交接也成了问题。stock_multi_repo 把研究流程拆成几块，尝试给数据、特征和评估留下相对清楚的边界。As downloaders, indicators and backtests grow, their interfaces become part of the research problem. stock_multi_repo separates the workflow into services with clearer boundaries around data, features and evaluation.

01

持仓、成本与净值Holdings, costs and NAV

Rnet,ₜ = Σᵢ wᵢ,ₜ₋₁ rᵢ,ₜ − costₜ costₜ ≈ c · Σᵢ |wtarget,ᵢ,ₜ − wpre,ᵢ,ₜ| Vₜ = Vₜ₋₁ (1 + Rnet,ₜ)

通用收盘到收盘记账示意：w 为上一期持仓权重，r 为资产收益；现金须单独纳入。c 为每单位成交额的单边成本率，wpre 为交易前漂移权重。实际成交时点、费用和滑点需由回测配置细化。General close-to-close accounting: w is prior-period exposure and r asset return; include cash explicitly. c is cost per traded notional and wpre pre-trade drifted weights. Execution timing, fees and slippage require the backtest configuration.

02

风险与增长指标Risk and growth measures

CAGR = (Vₜ / V₀)^(A/T) − 1 σannual = s(Rnet) √A MDD = maxₜ [1 − Vₜ / maxᵤ≤ₜ Vᵤ]

T 为收益期数，A 为每年期数；CAGR 要求首尾净值为正。MDD 采用正损失幅度，与负值回撤序列区别。所有指标需注明区间、频率、成本及基准。T counts return periods and A periods per year; CAGR requires positive initial/final NAV. MDD is a positive loss magnitude, unlike a negative drawdown series. Report interval, frequency, costs and benchmark.

RESEARCH COMPANION

把一次实验，留成可重来的过程。Keep an experiment reproducible.

阅读项目时，先沿着输入与输出走一遍，再展开值得追问的细节。以下是验证思路，不表示这些检查已全部完成。Follow the inputs and outputs, then explore the questions below. These are validation approaches, not claims that every check has been completed.

02 / OUTPUT

带着什么离开What comes out

实验配置、运行记录、净值与评估报告，保留彼此关联。Linked experiment configuration, run records, NAV and evaluation reports.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01同一组参数，为什么重跑结果不同？Why can the same parameters give a different run?+

逐项比对数据修订、依赖版本、随机种子与成交假设。参数相同不代表输入与环境相同。Compare data revisions, dependency versions, random seeds and execution assumptions. Matching parameters do not establish matching inputs or environments.

02增量计算与全量计算一致吗？Do incremental and full calculations agree?+

从指标最长回看期之前补足历史，并比较重叠区间的输出。跨日边界、缺失值和特征版本变化应单独检查。Provide enough history before the longest feature lookback and compare overlapping outputs. Review day boundaries, missing values and feature-version changes separately.

研究边界Research scope

这是服务化研究工程的整理，未在本次任务中验证各服务联调或生产部署。采集文档包含模拟数据回退设计，研究时需要明确数据来源。This describes a service-oriented research workspace, not verified production deployment or end-to-end integration. Ingestion documentation includes a simulated-data fallback; research needs explicit data provenance.
