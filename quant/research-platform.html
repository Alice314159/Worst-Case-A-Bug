VESPER / RESEARCH SERVICES

把一条研究流程，拆成能接上的小块。Give each part of the research a place.

当下载、指标和回测逐渐长大，项目之间如何交接也成了问题。stock_multi_repo 把研究流程拆成几块，尝试给数据、特征和评估留下相对清楚的边界。As downloaders, indicators and backtests grow, their interfaces become part of the research problem. stock_multi_repo separates the workflow into services with clearer boundaries around data, features and evaluation.

01

采集与同步Ingestion and sync

组织 ingest_service、sync_service 与共享 schema，连接股票列表和行情数据。Organize ingest_service, sync_service and shared schemas around stock lists and price data.

02

特征计算Feature computation

feature-service 文档描述技术指标、统计和时间特征，以及版本化与增量计算接口。feature-service documents technical, statistical and time features, with versioning and incremental computation interfaces.

03

回测与策略Backtests and strategies

backtest_service 文档覆盖成本、滑点、组合和滚动分析，analysis 中另有 ETF 轮动研究。backtest_service documents costs, slippage, portfolios and walk-forward analysis; analysis also contains ETF rotation research.

研究边界Research scope

这是服务化研究工程的整理，未在本次任务中验证各服务联调或生产部署。采集文档包含模拟数据回退设计，研究时需要明确数据来源。This describes a service-oriented research workspace, not verified production deployment or end-to-end integration. Ingestion documentation includes a simulated-data fallback; research needs explicit data provenance.
