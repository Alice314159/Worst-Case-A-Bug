VESPER / HMM RESEARCH

市场换了语气，方法也要重新听。Listen for a change in market regime.

与其急着猜下一根 K 线，我更想知道，眼前的行情与之前有什么不同。HMM 系列围绕市场状态展开：训练模型，观察状态，再研究它与仓位和风险过滤的关系。Rather than treating every market period alike, this series asks what has changed. The HMM projects train regime models, inspect their states and explore how those states relate to exposure and risk filters.

统一特征与状态识别Features and regimes

HMM3.0 使用统一特征管线与 GMM-HMM，研究牛市、震荡、熊市等语义状态。HMM3.0 uses a shared feature pipeline and GMM-HMM to study semantic labels such as bull, sideways and bear regimes.

02

滚动回测设计Rolling-test design

按训练／测试窗口滚动，逐日在线预测，并通过信号与结算日错位减少前视风险；另有参数敏感性研究。Roll through training/test folds, predict online day by day and separate signal and settlement timing to reduce look-ahead risk; parameter sensitivity is studied separately.

03

从状态到轮动From regime to rotation

hmm_rotation 将状态报告交给轮动逻辑，加入连续确认与风险过滤；HMM 保留早期特征和训练探索。hmm_rotation connects state reports to rotation logic, with confirmation periods and risk-off filters. HMM preserves earlier feature and training experiments.

METHOD / 公式与口径FORMULAS & CONVENTIONS

把计算的来路写清。Make the calculation explicit.

以下区分源码对应定义与通用解释。窗口、频率和参数以具体实验配置为准；所有信号只使用当时已可获得的数据。Implementation-linked definitions and general explanations are distinguished below. Windows, frequencies and parameters follow each experiment; signals must use only information available at the time.

01

状态转移与观测Transitions and emissions

aᵢⱼ = Pr(zₜ = j | zₜ₋₁ = i); Σⱼ aᵢⱼ = 1 bⱼ(x) = Σₘ cⱼₘ · N(x; μⱼₘ, Σⱼₘ)

z 为隐状态，x 为特征向量；c 为和为 1 的混合权重，μ、Σ 为高斯分量参数。状态编号没有天然的牛熊含义。z is a latent state and x a feature vector. Mixture weights c sum to one; μ and Σ parameterize Gaussian components. State IDs have no intrinsic bull/bear meaning.

02

在线过滤概率Online filtering

qₜ(j) ∝ bⱼ(xₜ) · Σᵢ qₜ₋₁(i) aᵢⱼ; Σⱼ qₜ(j) = 1

qₜ(j) = Pr(zₜ=j | x₁,…,xₜ)。这是只用截至 t 的信息的标准递推；全序列平滑或解码结果不可直接当成历史时点可交易信号。模型参数也须仅用当时训练集拟合。qₜ(j) conditions only on observations through t. This is the standard causal recursion; full-sequence smoothing or decoding is not a historical tradable signal. Model parameters must also be fitted only on the available training set.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01同样叫“状态 A”，是否还是同一个状态？Is state A the same after refitting?+

隐状态编号可能在重新拟合后置换。比较特征均值、波动与转移关系，再讨论语义标签；不要直接按编号拼接不同窗口。State IDs may permute after refitting. Compare feature means, volatility and transitions before aligning semantic labels across windows.

02概率改变时，是否立刻调仓？Should every probability change trigger a trade?+

把概率观察、连续确认和交易执行分开记录。验证阈值附近的反复切换与成本，再决定确认规则。Separate observation, confirmation and execution. Examine threshold oscillation and costs before setting confirmation rules.

研究边界Research scope

状态是模型对数据的归纳，名称不是市场事实。未来状态模拟与状态驱动策略都需要样本外复核。A regime is a model’s summary of data, not a market fact. State simulations and regime-driven strategies require out-of-sample review.
