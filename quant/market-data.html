VESPER / VESPER FETCH DATA

把研究的起点，放回数据里。Research starts with the data.

回测之前，先把每天要用的数据整理好。这条流水线从指数成分股出发，把分红送转、历史行情与交易时段的数据接在一起，让下游研究有一个可以反复回来的起点。Before a backtest comes the less visible work of preparing the data. This pipeline starts with index constituents and connects corporate actions, historical prices and trading-session updates into a shared research input.

把计算的来路写清。Make the calculation explicit.

01

收益与复权口径Returns and adjustment basis

rₜ = P*ₜ / P*ₜ₋₁ − 1; ℓₜ = ln(P*ₜ / P*ₜ₋₁) P*ₜ = Prawₜ · Fₜ / Fanchor

r 为简单收益，ℓ 为对数收益；F 是以乘法定义的复权因子，anchor 为归一化锚点。这是通用表达，实际因子方向及前复权锚点须遵循数据源。不要在复权收益上再重复加分红。r is simple return and ℓ log return. F is a multiplicative adjustment factor normalized to an anchor. This general expression requires verifying the vendor’s factor convention and anchor. Do not add dividends twice to adjusted returns.

02

数据完整性Data completeness

Coverage = Nvalid / Nexpected

Nexpected 来自标的有效上市区间及交易日历；Nvalid 为其中去重且通过字段校验的有效记录数。停牌、缺失和未上市须分别标记，完整性并不等于数值准确性。Expected observations follow the listing interval and trading calendar. Valid observations are deduplicated and field-validated. Distinguish suspensions, missing data and pre-listing periods; completeness does not establish accuracy.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01前后两次下载为什么不同？Why did a later download change history?+

区分源数据修订、复权锚点变化和本地处理差异。比较未复权价格、事件记录与因子，再解释差异。Separate vendor revisions, adjustment-anchor changes and processing differences. Compare raw prices, events and factors before attributing a discrepancy.

明确最后一条完整日线与未收盘快照的边界，检查时区、重复日期和字段单位。未收盘值不能悄悄覆盖已验证历史。Define the boundary between completed bars and intraday snapshots. Check timezone, duplicate dates and units; do not silently overwrite validated history with incomplete observations.
