VESPER / CORPORATE ACTIONS

一笔分红，也要把来处记清。Keep the history behind each dividend.

行情里的一次跳变，有时来自一笔分红。这个项目把不同来源的分红送转记录放在一起，做融合、校验和回溯，为后续的数据处理留下一份更清楚的底稿。A discontinuity in a price series can begin with a dividend. This project brings corporate-action records together, with merging, validation and historical follow-up to support later data work.

多源融合Source reconciliation

接入东方财富、巨潮资讯与门户数据兜底，合并分红送转记录。Combine EastMoney, CNInfo and portal fallback records.

在进入主表前执行字段与记录质量校验，减少来源差异对下游的影响。Check fields and record quality before merging into the master table.

支持增量更新及历史未实施记录回溯，持续维护事件状态。Support incremental updates and follow-up on historical actions that have not been implemented.

METHOD / 公式与口径FORMULAS & CONVENTIONS

把计算的来路写清。Make the calculation explicit.

以下区分源码对应定义与通用解释。窗口、频率和参数以具体实验配置为准；所有信号只使用当时已可获得的数据。Implementation-linked definitions and general explanations are distinguished below. Windows, frequencies and parameters follow each experiment; signals must use only information available at the time.

01

每股口径换算Per-share normalization

d = D₁₀ / 10; s = S₁₀ / 10; q = Q₁₀ / 10

将每 10 股现金分红、送转股和配股数量分别换成每股 d、s、q。先核实原始字段单位、税前税后与实施状态；方案不能直接当成已实施事件。Convert cash dividend, bonus/split shares and rights shares per ten shares into per-share d, s and q. Verify units, tax basis and implementation status before using a proposed action.

02

理论除权参考价Theoretical ex-rights reference

Pex = (Ppre − d + q·K) / (1 + s + q)

通用权益守恒示意：Ppre 为除权前参考价，K 为配股价；无配股时 q=0。适用于已统一每股口径的简单组合，交易所实际规则、舍入和特殊事件另行处理；不是主表融合算法。General value-conservation illustration: Ppre is the pre-action reference and K the rights subscription price; q=0 without rights. Exchange rules, rounding and special actions require separate treatment. This is not the record-merging algorithm.

RESEARCH COMPANION

合并记录，也保留不同的声音。Reconcile records without losing evidence.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01两条相似记录，是重复还是变更？Duplicate event or revised proposal?+

将标的、事件年度、关键日期与状态一起比较。修订方案和实施公告可能属于同一事件的不同阶段，不应只按金额去重。Compare instrument, event year, key dates and status together. A revised proposal and implementation notice may be stages of one event; amount alone is not a deduplication key.

02什么时候可以影响历史行情？When should an action affect prices?+

以已核实的实施状态和除权日期为依据，保留原始单位与来源。公告时间用于可获得性判断，不能代替除权日期。Use verified implementation status and ex-date, retaining source units. Announcement time determines information availability, not the ex-date itself.

研究边界Research scope

多来源并不自动等于准确。事件状态与关键日期需要结合原始公告复核。Multiple sources do not guarantee accuracy. Event status and important dates need checking against original announcements.
