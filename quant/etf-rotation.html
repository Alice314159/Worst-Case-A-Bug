VESPER / ETF ROTATION

换一种尺度，再看动量。Look at momentum across horizons.

同样叫动量，计算的方式不同，看到的市场也会不同。这个项目把几种定义放在同一条研究流程里，先比较，再把参数放到后面的时间里检验。Momentum looks different depending on how it is measured. This project compares several definitions in one workflow, then tests chosen parameters on later periods.

01

四种动量定义Four momentum definitions

比较回归动量、简单动量、12–1 动量与风险调整动量。Compare regression, simple, 12–1 and risk-adjusted momentum.

02

参数研究Parameter research

支持网格搜索、贝叶斯优化及多组窗口组合，研究参数对结果的影响。Use grid search, Bayesian optimization and window combinations to explore parameter sensitivity.

03

滚动验证与评估Rolling validation

支持 Walk-forward 分析，并记录波动、回撤、换手和交易成本等指标。Support walk-forward analysis and evaluate volatility, drawdown, turnover and transaction costs.

研究边界Research scope

参数搜索可能拟合历史噪声。滚动测试只是验证过程的一部分，不能证明未来有效；这里不展示收益承诺。Parameter search can fit historical noise. Rolling tests are one part of validation and do not establish future effectiveness. No performance promise is presented here.
