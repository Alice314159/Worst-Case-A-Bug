VESPER / ETF ROTATION

换一种尺度，再看动量。Look at momentum across horizons.

同样叫动量，计算的方式不同，看到的市场也会不同。这个项目把几种定义放在同一条研究流程里，先比较，再把参数放到后面的时间里检验。Momentum looks different depending on how it is measured. This project compares several definitions in one workflow, then tests chosen parameters on later periods.

01

四种动量定义Four momentum definitions

比较回归动量、简单动量、12–1 动量与风险调整动量。Compare regression, simple, 12–1 and risk-adjusted momentum.

02

参数研究Parameter research

支持网格搜索、贝叶斯优化及多组窗口组合，研究参数对结果的影响。Use grid search, Bayesian optimization and window combinations to explore parameter sensitivity.

03

滚动验证与评估Rolling validation

支持 Walk-forward 分析，并记录波动、回撤、换手和交易成本等指标。Support walk-forward analysis and evaluate volatility, drawdown, turnover and transaction costs.

METHOD / 公式与口径FORMULAS & CONVENTIONS

把计算的来路写清。Make the calculation explicit.

以下区分源码对应定义与通用解释。窗口、频率和参数以具体实验配置为准；所有信号只使用当时已可获得的数据。Implementation-linked definitions and general explanations are distinguished below. Windows, frequencies and parameters follow each experiment; signals must use only information available at the time.

01

简单动量Simple momentum

M(t, L) = Pₜ / Pₜ₋ₗ − 1

P 为一致复权口径的价格，L 为回看期；月频使用月末价格。P is a consistently adjusted price; L is the lookback. Monthly calculations use month-end prices.

02

回归动量Regression momentum

ln Pⱼ = a + b·j + εⱼ; S = A·b·R²

对窗口内对数价格做线性回归。b 为每期斜率，A 为每年期数；R² = 1 − Σ残差² / Σ(ln P − 均值)²。源码使用 A·b·R²，不是指数化收益；平坦或无效窗口不评分。Fit log prices over the window. b is slope per period, A periods per year; R² = 1 − residual sum of squares / total sum of squares. The implementation uses A·b·R², not an exponentiated return; invalid or flat windows are excluded.

03

跳月动量Skip-month momentum

M(t, L, s) = Pₜ₋ₛ / Pₜ₋ₛ₋ₗ − 1

按月末取价，s 为跳过的最近月份，L 为实际收益窗口。源码 L=12、s=1 时分母是 Pₜ₋₁₃；常见 Pₜ₋₁ / Pₜ₋₁₂ − 1 则对应 L=11、s=1。Use month-end prices; s skips recent months and L is the return window. L=12, s=1 uses Pₜ₋₁₃ as denominator. The conventional Pₜ₋₁ / Pₜ₋₁₂ − 1 instead corresponds to L=11, s=1.

04

风险调整与多窗口Risk adjustment and window aggregation

Rₐ = (Pₜ / Pₜ₋ₙ)^(A/n) − 1; Sᵣ = (Rₐ − r_f) / (sᵣ√A) Sᵢ = Σₖ ωₖ Mᵢ,ₖ; Σₖ ωₖ = 1

n 为日收益观测数，sᵣ 为日收益样本标准差（ddof=1），r_f 表示年化无风险利率。ω 为归一化窗口权重；风险调整评分另受波动下限和可选截断约束。n counts daily returns; sᵣ is their sample standard deviation (ddof=1); r_f denotes the annual risk-free rate. ω are normalized window weights. Volatility floors and optional clipping also apply.

DESIGN NOTES / 设计推演DESIGN STUDY

把配置、运行和复查接起来。Connect setup, execution and review.

从现有项目的功能出发，梳理操作顺序与信息层次。以下界面是为介绍项目绘制的设计示意，不是运行截图。These design studies organize the existing project’s capabilities into a workflow and information hierarchy. The interface below is an illustrative concept, not a running application screenshot.

  1. 01

    明确比较对象Define the comparison

    先固定研究问题，再决定候选动量与窗口组合。Fix the question before choosing momentum methods and window combinations.

  2. 02

    分开训练与检验Separate fitting and testing

    参数搜索与后续验证分开呈现，保留实验的时间边界。Separate parameter search from later validation, preserving time boundaries.

  3. 03

    留下运行记录Keep run records

    记录配置、任务与输出，让下一次比较有据可循。Keep configuration, jobs and outputs together for later comparisons.

配置分层Layer the configuration

基础设置、训练参数和风控分别组织，减少单页负担。Group setup, training and risk controls instead of crowding one form.

运行与历史并列Keep runs near history

把队列和历史记录留在近处，方便复查实验差异。Keep queues and history nearby to review differences between experiments.

RESEARCH COMPANION

从一个排名，走到一套可检查的规则。Turn a ranking into an auditable rule.

阅读项目时，先沿着输入与输出走一遍，再展开值得追问的细节。以下是验证思路，不表示这些检查已全部完成。Follow the inputs and outputs, then explore the questions below. These are validation approaches, not claims that every check has been completed.

01 / INPUT

从什么开始What goes in

当时可交易的 ETF 池、价格窗口、动量方法、选取和调仓设置。The tradable ETF universe, price windows, momentum method, selection and rebalance settings.

↘
02 / OUTPUT

带着什么离开What comes out

评分排序、目标权重、调仓记录与样本外评估；保持时间顺序。Score rankings, target weights, rebalance records and out-of-sample evaluation in time order.

再往下问一步QUESTIONS TO TAKE FURTHER

01排名变化，是否一定需要换仓？Does a ranking change require a trade?+

同时检查调仓日、入选规则、现金处理和交易限制。评分是选择输入，不能代替完整的执行规则。Check rebalance dates, selection rules, cash treatment and trading constraints. A score is an input to selection, not a complete execution rule.

02最优窗口在别的区间仍然好吗？Does the chosen window generalize?+

把参数选择限制在训练区间，在后续区间验证；同时观察邻近参数与成本敏感性。只展示最佳单组参数容易掩盖不稳定性。Select parameters on training periods and test later periods. Inspect nearby settings and cost sensitivity; one best parameter set can hide instability.

研究边界Research scope

参数搜索可能拟合历史噪声。滚动测试只是验证过程的一部分，不能证明未来有效；这里不展示收益承诺。Parameter search can fit historical noise. Rolling tests are one part of validation and do not establish future effectiveness. No performance promise is presented here.
