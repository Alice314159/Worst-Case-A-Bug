01顿悟时刻Moments of insight

Aha，原来如此！Aha! Now It Makes Sense

筹备中In preparation+

有些道理，听过很多遍，却在某个普通的瞬间才真正懂得。想聊聊那些迟来的领悟：是什么，让零散的经历忽然连成了一条线？Sometimes an idea we have heard many times only makes sense in an ordinary moment. What turns scattered experiences into a connection we can finally see?

02注意力与日常Attention in everyday life

被偷走的注意力Our Stolen Attention

筹备中In preparation+

只是想看一眼手机，回过神却过去了很久。那些被消息、推荐和不断切换切碎的时间里，我们失去了什么，又能怎样把注意力慢慢带回来？A quick glance at a phone becomes much longer. What do we lose when notifications, recommendations and constant switching fragment our time, and how might we begin to reclaim our attention?
