02 — 量化笔记Quant notes

先想办法证伪，再谈信心。Try to break it first.

只写方法，不放收益数字，也不构成建议。 Method only — no performance numbers, no advice.

A

HMM：先承认市场会换性格HMM: the market changes personality

用隐马尔可夫把行情切成几个状态，不是为了预测下一根 K 线，而是想知道「现在这套逻辑还适用吗」。状态判错的代价，往往比信号弱一点更贵。 I use a hidden Markov model to cut the tape into regimes — not to predict the next candle, but to ask whether the logic I'm running still applies. Misreading the regime usually costs more than a weak signal does.

B

动量与回归的那条线Where momentum becomes reversal

同一个信号，换个时间尺度就换个方向。短周期像回归，长周期像动量，中间那段最贵。我做的大部分工作就是反复量这条线在哪。 The same signal flips sign when the horizon changes: short looks like reversal, long looks like momentum, and the middle is expensive. Most of my work is measuring where that line sits.

C

一个想法怎么走完流程How an idea gets tested

假设 → 构造 → 分层检验 → 加上成本和容量 → 小仓位跟一段。任何一步说不通就归档，不硬凑。 Hypothesis, construction, quantile test, costs and capacity, then small-size tracking. If any step doesn't hold up, it gets archived — not forced.

归档的想法比留下的多得多，这一点我觉得挺健康。 Far more ideas get archived than kept. I've come to think that's healthy.
