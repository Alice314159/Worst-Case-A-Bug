01 / Muir Woods · 2026

Rings of Time and Light

Sunlight falls
through a thousand years of leaves,
waving a soft mist in the air.

I lift my head
and see the sharp edge of light,
cutting through silence.

In the slow breeze of time,
the redwood stands—
its trunk laid open,
rings unfolding
like quiet stories.

Eighty—
the world begins to count.
One hundred and five—
paper is born in the East,
hands shaping words
from pulp and fiber.

And still,
this tree was only learning
how to reach the sky.

Now their paths meet—
ink and green,
salt and root.
I trace the lines in the wood,
feeling the pulse of centuries.

Time does not pass.
It rests,
softly kept
by the forest.
