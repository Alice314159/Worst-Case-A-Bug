SMALL EXPERIMENTS / 探索新事物TRYING SOMETHING NEW

有些好奇，先上手再说。Some curiosities begin with trying.

新语言、新城市、新工具、新录音设备——先上手试，做坏了再说。大不了就出个 Bug。New languages, new cities, new tools, new recording gear. Try them, see what happens, and work through the mistakes. Worst case, a bug.

影像尝试A DIFFERENT VIEW

Insta360

换一个视角，看平常走过的路。想试着把风景之外的片刻也留下：转身时的光，走路时的节奏，还有镜头外正在发生的事。A different way of looking at a familiar path. A chance to keep the moments around the landscape too: the light while turning, the rhythm of a walk, and what happens just outside the frame.

声音尝试LEARNING TO LISTEN

猛犸 · 录音设备Recording gear · 猛犸

把注意力交给耳朵。试着听清一句话里的停顿，听见房间里的回声，也听见风经过麦克风的声音。设备一点点摸熟，想说的话也慢慢找到自己的节奏。Let the ears take the lead. Notice a pause in a sentence, the echo of a room, the wind reaching a microphone. As the gear becomes familiar, the words can find their own rhythm.
