以 South Kaibab 至 Ooh Aah Point 作为路线参考：从南缘沿山脊下行，回程再爬升。记录的是这一折返点的往返距离，不是走到谷底。South Kaibab to Ooh Aah Point is the reference route: descend from the South Rim, then climb back out. The distance is for this turnaround point, not the canyon floor.

路线沿途无供水、遮阴少，返程需要爬升；出发前查看水源、天气与关闭公告。There is no water along the trail and little shade; the return is uphill. Review water, weather and closure notices before setting out.

Lost Mine Trail 是这里的一条山地路线参考，沿 Chisos Mountains 上升，经过松树、橡树和杜松生长的山地，抵达开阔山脊。Lost Mine Trail is a mountain-route reference in the Chisos Mountains, ascending through pine, oak and juniper terrain toward an open ridge.

干燥炎热，停车位有限；出发前查看园内道路、步道开放情况与天气。Expect a hot, dry climate and limited parking. Check road/trail access and weather before departure.
